Frequently Asked Questions

Have a common question? Find the answer here.

Q: How do I register for an upcoming event?

Event registration details will be available on our Events page and through our newsletter. Once events are published, you'll be able to register directly through their detail pages.

Q: How can I plan an event with Social Mate?

Contact us through this form with "Event Planning" as your inquiry type, or visit our Services page for more information about our event management offerings.

Q: Can I sponsor or become a partner?

Yes! We're always interested in partnerships and sponsorship opportunities. Please select "Sponsorships" or "Partnerships" in the inquiry type and tell us more about your interest.

Q: How do I stay updated on new events?

Subscribe to our newsletter on our homepage or Events page to receive announcements about upcoming events, networking opportunities, and special experiences.

Q: Do you offer corporate event packages?

Yes! We provide comprehensive corporate event planning and management. Contact us to discuss your specific needs and requirements.
