How We Work

At Social Mate, we believe in flexibility and opportunity. Choose the work arrangement that suits you best:

On-Site

Work from our Dubai office and be part of the team culture. Perfect for roles requiring direct collaboration and hands-on event management.

  • Full team collaboration
  • Professional environment
  • Direct mentorship

Remote

Work from anywhere while staying connected. Ideal for strategic planning, content creation, and independent project work.

  • Work from home comfort
  • Flexible schedule
  • Work-life balance

Hybrid

Best of both worlds - split your time between office and remote work. Great for roles combining strategy and execution.

  • Flexibility & collaboration
  • Strategic focus days
  • Office connection time

Commission-Based Sales

Entrepreneurial opportunity for sales-driven professionals. Build your income based on performance and success.

  • Unlimited earning potential
  • Performance-based rewards
  • Entrepreneurial freedom
